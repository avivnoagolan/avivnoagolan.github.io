Introduction

The Modern Arena: Brand Identity in Sports

This magazine explores the logos, uniforms, campaigns, typography, motion graphics, and visual storytelling behind today’s biggest sporting events. From the Olympics to the World Cup, this publication is for readers interested in how sports become brands people live through.
